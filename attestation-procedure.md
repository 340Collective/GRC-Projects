# 340 Collective acknowledgment procedure

**Version:** 0.2.0

**Prepared:** 2026-10-15

**Owner:** Faris N., Strategy & Operations Lead (proposed policy assignment; acceptance pending)

## Record only genuine responses

Use the workbook's **Attestations** sheet. Its columns are exactly:

| Team Member | Policy ID | Version | Date Acknowledged | Method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Leave blank until the collaborator is identified | One policy ID | Exact version read | Actual date of the collaborator's response | Channel and restricted evidence reference |

This example explains the fields and is not a completed record. Starter rows in the workbook contain the six draft IDs and version 0.2.0 only. Replace the versions with the approved release before seeking policy acknowledgment. Never treat a prefilled policy ID as an acknowledgment.

## Collection workflow

1. Confirm the in-scope roster with the Studio Lead. Include the lead, approver, contractors and other collaborators who handle 340 Collective information. The complete lab collaborator roster has not been supplied. Public leadership names do not establish the lab participant list.
2. Confirm that the exact policy version is approved and available to the collaborator. Supply a stable release/commit reference with the request.
3. Ask each person for their own explicit acknowledgment. Proposed deadline: before granting new access, or within 10 business days of an approved substantive update for existing collaborators.
4. Verify identity against the roster and retain the original response in restricted storage. Acceptable methods include a verified email reply, authenticated form, attributed repository discussion comment, signed document or a recorded meeting statement attributable to that individual. Attendance or a read receipt alone is insufficient.
5. Create one row per person, policy and version. If a response explicitly acknowledges all six listed policies, enter six rows with the same genuine response date and evidence reference. Do not infer consent from a general project approval.
6. Enter a Method such as `Email — restricted evidence record ACK-2026-001`. The reference must resolve for an authorised reviewer. Do not paste the whole message, signature or personal address into a public tracker.
7. Check the roster against the six current approved versions. Flag missing evidence, unanswered requests, duplicate rows and acknowledgments for superseded versions. Preserve historical records separately; do not overwrite them when a new version is issued.
8. Follow up once at the deadline and raise unresolved access decisions with the Studio Lead. Record an extension explicitly; it is not acknowledgment. Do not assume compliance from silence.

>
> Name: [collaborator completes]
>
> Policy IDs and versions: [list each exact approved policy/version]
>
> Release or commit reference: [reference]
>
> Date: [actual response date]
>
> Questions or clarification needed: [optional]

The individual supplies this acknowledgment. Do not fill their name, date or statement on their behalf. A material unresolved question must be addressed before the owner treats the acknowledgment as complete.

## Evidence and privacy

The Operations Lead controls access to the roster, tracker and originals. Apply the Data Retention Policy to identity-linked attestations. Store minimal references in Git only where the repository's access and retention are suitable. For a public portfolio, publish an empty or anonymised tracker and a truthful completion summary; do not expose collaborators' personal evidence.
