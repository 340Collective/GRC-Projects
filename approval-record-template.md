Each approval must identify the exact content approved. A reviewer or approver completes their own decision; a drafting assistant does not sign on their behalf. If the reviewer and approver are the same person, record why and any additional peer review.

Keep completed records in the agreed restricted team location. Add a minimal reference to each policy change log. Do not mark a policy approved based only on this blank template, a scheduled meeting, a Git commit or a draft review comment.
