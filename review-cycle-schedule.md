## Triggered reviews

| Policy | Examples of a major change |
| --- | --- |
| Information Security | New production service, studio/crew model, client market, storage/delivery platform or significant incident. |
| Acceptable Use | Changed AI support, filming/editing tools, device arrangements, media permissions or public production promises. |
| Access Control | New AWS/identity design, media-sharing method, client social/ad/website service, or emergency-access arrangement. |
| Vendor Management | New cloud/payment/media provider, partner arrangement, processing location or supplier incident. |
| Incident Response | Lost shoot media, unauthorised publication, client-account compromise, response-team change or notification obligation. |
| Data Retention | Changed raw-media/log commitment, delivery/archive workflow, newsletter, contract, tax requirement or jurisdiction. |

Start a triggered review within 10 business days of identification, sooner for urgent risk. Record event date, scope, reviewer, agreed completion date and interim controls. Bring the workbook's Next Review Date forward when that completion date is earlier; preserve the previous annual date in the review record. Review affected companion policies together.

## Monthly and annual routine

- **Monthly:** the Operations Lead checks upcoming/overdue reviews, role acceptance and outstanding acknowledgments.
- **30 days before due:** the owner opens a review record and gathers relevant production, incident, access, supplier and retention evidence.
- **By the due date:** record changed/no-change outcome, open issues and the approver's decision. Update the change log and next review date; update the effective date where appropriate.
- **After approval:** publish the exact version and communicate changed obligations. Collect new acknowledgments for substantive changes. A documented no-change review does not automatically require re-attestation.
- **If overdue:** keep the original deadline visible; record the reason and escalate to the Studio Lead for a recovery date and interim decision. An overdue review is not complete.

The workbook has editable **Approval Status** and **Last Review Completed** fields. Populate them only from real decisions and review evidence. Preserve that evidence with [the approval record template](approval-record-template.md). The [website alignment note](website-alignment.md) records source conflicts to resolve. No calendar reminders or messages are created by this schedule.
