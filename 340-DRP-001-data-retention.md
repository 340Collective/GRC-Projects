## 1. Purpose and scope

340 Collective must retain information for a defined purpose, then securely delete or irreversibly anonymise it. Cover authoritative stores and copies in forms, messages, email, capture media, editing devices, cloud storage, delivery services, suppliers, archives and backups.

The [published FAQ](https://340collective.com/faq) describes raw-file retention of around 60 days after delivery and access logs kept up to 90 days. This draft adopts a 60-day operational raw-media default and a 90-day maximum for ordinary access logs. Other periods below are proposed internal choices; they are not published guarantees or universal statutory requirements. The Operations Lead must confirm purpose, feasibility, applicable U.S. requirements and client agreements before approval.

## 3. Production, contact and billing rules

The studio owns its produced work and describes licences to clients in its published terms. Retention must still follow purpose, permissions, privacy and the applicable agreement. Ownership does not justify retaining all identifiable media indefinitely. Client-supplied footage and brand assets need their own rights/purpose record and the applicable project retention rule.

Record delivery and disposal dates per project. If a raw-file purchase or agreed extension is requested, record the decision and revised expiry before deletion; do not promise recovery from files already deleted. Keep an active or disputed project open only with a documented purpose and review date.

The contact form collects contact details, social handles and project/brand information. Do not request property secrets, identity documents or card data there. When an enquiry becomes a project, retain only the information needed for the relevant project and contractual purpose. Marketing subscription is a separate choice.

Replace the previous UK-limited-company rationale with U.S. recordkeeping assessment. The proposed seven-year accounting period is an internal baseline, not a claim that all U.S. invoices legally require seven years. The [IRS guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records) explains that periods depend on the record and circumstances and that other purposes may require longer retention. Confirm entity type, tax treatment, financial year and any asset, employment-tax, audit or dispute requirements before adopting the rule.

## 4. Disposal, rights requests and holds

Review upcoming expiries monthly and schedule deletion by the applicable expiry; do not add a routine grace period to the 60-day media default or 90-day log maximum. Record category, date range, locations, action date, actor and verification without copying deleted personal content into the log. Proposed disposal-log retention is two years.

The published route for access/deletion requests is help@340collective.com. Verify the requester proportionately, identify the records and assess applicable rights and retention obligations. Do not promise deletion that cannot be demonstrated, or treat customer-service response times as legal deadlines.

The Studio Lead may approve a scoped legal, investigation or dispute hold with reason, owner, start and review date. Review holds quarterly and dispose of already-expired records promptly after release. An active investigation can preserve necessary evidence; it does not justify extending every record's retention.

Delete recoverable bins, exports, object versions, local media and supplier copies where applicable. Routine backup data must age out within the shorter of the approved backup lifetime and the category limit. Restrict expired data from restoration and reapply valid deletion actions before restored systems resume service. Document any inability to meet a published limit as a gap requiring correction, not as silent compliance.

Do not put client data, codes, raw media or real acknowledgment evidence into a public Git repository. Removing a working file does not erase history or clones. Escalate unintended exposure and verify authorised removal where feasible.

## 5. Review

Review annually and when collection, services, permissions, provider storage, geography, tax/contract requirements or public commitments change. The [website alignment note](../governance/website-alignment.md) records confirmed statements and remaining decisions. An internal exception cannot silently override a public commitment or applicable law.
