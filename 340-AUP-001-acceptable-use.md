## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy covers the use of 340 Collective accounts, devices, cameras, storage media, editing tools and collaboration services by the team and directly managed crew. It applies on location and during planning, editing, delivery and approved client-account work, including work on personally owned devices.

## 2. Human production and appropriate tool use

The [About page and FAQ](../governance/website-alignment.md) describe human-led creative production. Client media must be scripted, shot, edited and delivered by people. Do not substitute AI-generated media or an automated production service for that commitment. The FAQ permits limited AI assistance for ideas, analytics and research; it does not permit unrestricted use of client material in AI tools.

Use only approved tools for those limited purposes. Do not upload unreleased footage, client briefs, identifiable images, property security details or credentials to an unapproved AI service. Approval must cover the specific data, purpose, provider retention and permitted use. Check outputs and preserve human accountability. Do not describe generated notes, assessments or acknowledgments as observed or human-signed evidence.

The public model is the studio's own team and directly managed crew. Do not send creative work to an independent outsourcing or offshore production service as a convenience. A proposed change to that model requires business review of the affected client and public commitments before any use.

## 3. Devices, capture and sharing

- Use individual accounts, unique passwords, a password manager and required MFA. Do not share credentials or approve unexpected MFA requests.
- Use supported devices, security updates, disk encryption and a screen lock of no more than five minutes for devices that support it. For cameras or removable media without encryption, maintain physical custody, minimise storage time and transfer promptly to approved encrypted storage.
- Keep memory cards, drives and devices secure during travel and filming. Do not leave them unattended in a vehicle or give an unauthorised person access to a shoot folder.
- Capture only the agreed property or subject. Avoid documents, personal screens, alarm panels, keys and other incidental private details. Verify required client permissions. A signed parent/guardian consent is required before capturing or delivering identifiable footage of a minor, consistent with the published FAQ.
- Separate projects and use approved delivery tools. Check recipients, links, file contents and publishing authority. Never post behind-the-scenes or portfolio material merely because you can access it.
- Use approved or properly licensed music, imagery, software and source assets for the intended use. Do not remove licence or consent evidence needed to support a delivery.

## 4. Communications, accounts and records

Keep client decisions, scope approvals and delivery evidence in the agreed project record. Do not move them into a private account that the studio cannot manage. Record billing or scope changes in writing through the authorised workflow.

Use delegated client social, page, advertising or website permissions only for a contracted service and only within its approved purpose and budget. Where social management is not included, delivery of content does not authorise posting or access to the client's performance data.

Do not collect payment-card details in forms, messages, spreadsheets or footage. Use the approved processor flow once its configuration is confirmed. Do not add a contact-form enquiry to the optional newsletter without the appropriate subscription choice; honour unsubscribe requests promptly.

Do not scan or test external systems without written asset-owner permission and scope. This manual lab grants no production-testing authority. Use synthetic client and media data in lab evidence; restrict real collaborator attestations.

## 5. Reporting, departure and oversight

Report a missing card/device, exposed key, wrong recipient, premature listing release or suspicious client-account activity immediately. The published reporting address is help@340collective.com; use the agreed urgent internal route as well. Routine customer-response windows must not delay security escalation.

On departure or project completion, hand over records and return or remove working copies under [Data Retention](340-DRP-001-data-retention.md). Access revocation is a separate administrative step and must be verified. Temporary downloads must be removed within seven days of task completion unless an approved record rule or hold applies.

The Operations Lead may review relevant business security logs proportionately and with appropriate notice. This does not authorise unrestricted inspection of private material on a personal device. The Studio Lead reviews policy breaches and access restrictions. Use the [Information Security](340-ISP-001-information-security.md) exception process; do not approve your own exception.

Review annually and after changes in production, tools, client-account services or AI use. Obtain real acknowledgments of newly approved substantive versions.
